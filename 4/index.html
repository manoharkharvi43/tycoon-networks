FAQ

Common questions

What is a Google Business Profile?

It is the free business listing that appears on Google Search and Google Maps, showing your address, phone number, photos, hours and reviews.

My business already has a listing. Can you still help?

Yes. We can help you claim and verify an existing listing, then optimize and manage it.

What do I receive every month?

Regular updates and posters on your profile, review and rating management, and a monthly performance report.

How do the QR codes work?

The location QR code opens your business on Google Maps, and the review QR code takes customers straight to your review page. You can print them at your counter or on packaging.
